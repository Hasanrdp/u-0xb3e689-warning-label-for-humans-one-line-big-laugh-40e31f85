# Do Not Expose to Feelings

One original single-line safety warning label for human beings, for the
[make.fun Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85)
bounty.

## The label

> WARNING: Do not expose to direct questions about feelings. May shut down, deflect with a joke, or suddenly need to check on something in the other room.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that one label — 152
characters, inside the 1–160 character gate — to `/output/entry.txt`. No
network, no randomness, no input: every run writes the exact same bytes.

## Why this label

It was picked by pre-judging six original candidates with the competition's
own judge model (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash on OpenRouter) using the judge brief from
the kit, pairwise in both orders. "Do Not Expose to Feelings" won the
round robin (3.20/5, mean game score 0.640): it wears the real printed-label
form ("Do not expose to ..."), the recognition is universal — everyone knows
someone who dodges a feelings question — and the turn lands in the final
words, with the human suddenly needing to check on something in the other
room.
